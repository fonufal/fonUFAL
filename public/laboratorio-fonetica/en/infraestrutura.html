01 / FACILITIES

Spaces, recording systems and experimental equipment.

Audio and video recording, digitisation of speech collections, acoustic analysis and experimental protocols.

01—05
01

Speech and audio recording

Controlled recordings, interviews and field recording.

For controlled speech recordings and fieldwork, the laboratory has an acoustic booth, TASCAM DR-40 and Marantz PMD661 portable digital recorders, and Sound Devices USBPre audio interfaces. Recording options include a Nady HM-10 headset microphone, an Audio-Technica ATR1200 dynamic microphone, two wireless handheld dynamic microphones and four Sennheiser AVX digital wireless lavalier systems. Deadcat windshields support recording outdoors.

02

Video recording and production

Video, synchronised sound and multicamera production, including equipment acquired through the CAPES Pró-Equipamentos programme.

The audiovisual setup includes three Blackmagic Design Cinema Camera 6K Full Frame cameras, Sony NXCAM professional camcorders, Panasonic SDR-SW20 compact camcorders, Sony Handycam HDR-PJ10 cameras and an Amcrest ProHD 2K IP camera. Multi-camera production is supported by a Blackmagic ATEM Television Studio HD8 ISO switcher and an ATEM Microphone Converter. The inventory also records a Sony camera lens without a specified model. Part of this equipment was acquired through the CAPES Pró-Equipamentos programme.

03

Documentation and preservation

Digitisation, annotation and organisation of speech archives.

For the digitisation and preservation of historical recordings, the laboratory has an Akai 4000DS Mk II reel-to-reel tape recorder and BASF magnetic tape reels. File storage and transfer are supported by a Western Digital Elements Portable external hard drive, SD and microSD memory cards and adapters, a TASCAM PS-P520E power supply, battery chargers and portable power sources. Speech collections are documented through time-aligned segmentation, transcription and annotation procedures.

04

Acoustic analysis and experimentation

Acoustic measurements, perception protocols and monitored playback.

For analysis and experimentation, the laboratory has computer peripherals including four Apple Wireless Keyboards and three Apple Magic Mice, along with two M-Audio BX8 D2 active audio monitors and an Aphex HeadPod 4 headphone amplifier. Six packs of fifty Etymotic ER1-14A disposable audiometry eartips are recorded for listening procedures. Speech data are processed using tools such as Praat and ELAN, with analysis and processing workflows in R and Python.

05

Technical support and accessories

Mounting, transport, power and connectivity equipment.

Technical support equipment includes four E-Image AT7402A professional tripods with GH03 heads, a Manfrotto MVH502AH Pro Video Head and additional supports for cameras, audio recorders and microphones. Transport and storage are supported by a Lowepro camera bag and a Platt Cases protective case. The infrastructure also includes an Agratto LIV Inverter 12,000 BTU air conditioner, memory cards, adapters, USB, P2/RCA audio, VGA, Ethernet and power cables, AC/DC power supplies, battery chargers and a power bank.
