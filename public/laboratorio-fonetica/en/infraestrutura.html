01 / FACILITIES

Infrastructure for recording and studying speech.

The items below reflect the partial inventory supported by available institutional documentation. Please confirm availability with the laboratory coordinator.

01—04
01

Controlled recording

An acoustic booth and speech capture equipment.

Acoustic booth; TASCAM DR-40/DR-40X and Marantz PMD661 recorders; DPA 4066 headset microphone and MicroDot-to-XLR adapter.

02

Documentation

Resources for organising, reviewing and annotating speech records.

Support for annotation, preparation of experimental stimuli, and organising study materials.

03

Phonetic analysis

Acoustic and prosodic methods for studying speech.

Relevant tools include Praat, ELAN, R/RStudio and Python; their use depends on the methodological design of each activity.

04

Auxiliary resources

Supporting items mentioned in the available documentation.

iPad and headphones, plus recording and monitoring accessories.

This is a public summary of a partial inventory, not a certification of maintenance, quantities, ownership, or immediate availability. Requested modernisation equipment is not presented as installed.
