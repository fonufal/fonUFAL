Research, teaching and documentation

The laboratory provides research infrastructure. Associated research groups and projects retain their own institutional identities, management and equipment inventories.

Institutional relationships

The Phonetics Laboratory is part of UFAL’s Faculty of Letters and is based at CEPEL. fonUFAL is a research group associated with this field; Lapelc² is a different laboratory. These are not alternative names for one unit.
