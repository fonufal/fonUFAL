RESEARCH AND MEMORY

Projects and experiments.

Initiatives associated with the development of phonetics and speech documentation research at UFAL.

01 / DOCUMENTATION

Yaathe documentation

Documentation of the Yaathe (Fulni-ô) language, including the organisation of oral recordings and preparation of research and teaching materials. Led by Januacele da Costa, with participation from Miguel Oliveira Jr. and collaborators.

Since 2010 · CNPq 475763/2010-6
02 / SPEECH ARCHIVE

NURC Digital

Preservation, digitisation and annotation of recordings from the NURC/Recife project, developing methods to make spoken-language collections available for study and analysis.

Since 2011 · CNPq 472918/2012-5
03 / PERCEPTION

Perception experiments

Listening experiments investigating how listeners perceive prosodic boundaries and cues in the organisation of spontaneous narratives, carried out with students and collaborators.

Research initiated in 2010

WHAT WE DO

Research, teaching and documentation

The laboratory provides research infrastructure. Associated research groups and projects retain their own institutional identities, management and equipment inventories.

Institutional relationships

The Phonetics Laboratory is part of UFAL’s Faculty of Letters and is based at CEPEL. fonUFAL is a research group associated with this field; Lapelc² is a different laboratory. These are not alternative names for one unit.
