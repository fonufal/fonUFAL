03 / RESEARCH

Research and documentation.

Phonetics, speech collections and perception.

PROJECTS AND EXPERIMENTS

The research combines speech documentation and archive analysis, recording in spontaneous and controlled settings, acoustic measurements and perception experiments. Areas of investigation include prosodic cues to discourse segmentation, narrative organisation, turn-taking and the relationship between listening and eye-fixation patterns.

01 / DOCUMENTATION

Yaathe language documentation

Recording, organising and documenting oral data in Yaathe (Fulni-ô), including the preparation of linguistic material for analysis and preservation. Led by Januacele da Costa, with participation by Miguel Oliveira Jr.

Language documentation project

02 / SPEECH ARCHIVES

NURC Digital

Digitisation of magnetic tape recordings, organisation, preservation and annotation of the NURC/Recife spoken-language collection, making recordings available for linguistic research.

Spoken-language archive

View project or academic record
03 / PERCEPTION

Prosodic perception in narratives

Listening experiments examine how people identify discourse units and event boundaries using pauses, fundamental frequency, duration and other prosodic cues.

Experimental research programme

04 / DOCTORAL THESIS · 2025

Remildo Barbosa da Silva

Investigation of prosody and eye-fixation patterns in turn transitions in spontaneous dyadic conversations. Acoustic measures and eye tracking are combined to examine the anticipation of speaker turns. Supervisor: Miguel Oliveira Jr.

PhD in Linguistics

05 / DOCTORAL THESIS · 2023

Ebson Wilkerson Rocha da Silva

The role of prosody in Portuguese discourse processing: an online-laboratory perception study. The thesis examines event segmentation and narrative comprehension. Supervisor: Miguel Oliveira Jr.

06 / MASTER'S THESIS · 2025

Arthur Ronald Brasil Terto

Prosody as a marker of event-based discourse segmentation: a perception study with eye tracking. Investigates prosodic cues in read narratives and how listeners process them. Supervisor: Aldir Santos de Paula; Miguel Oliveira Jr. served on the examination committee.
