02.2

Entre muitas vozes: síntese de fala usando dados de spontaneous speech com variedades linguísticas do português

CNPq project devoted to developing, evaluating and disseminating TTS technologies capable of representing the prosodic and accent diversity of Portuguese with greater naturalness and intelligibility. It combines harmonization of spontaneous-speech corpora, prosodic segmentation, synthesis models, EyeTrackingMOS and preparation for the Blizzard Challenge 2027.

02.3

Índice neurofisiológico implícito da naturalidade da fala sintetizada

CNPq Universal 2026 proposal investigating whether EEG signals recorded during listening without explicit ratings predict naturalness estimates from an independent panel and add information to contemporary audio-based models. The design compares audio, EEG and audio+EEG models and provides for open release of corpus data, protocol, code and metadata within ethical constraints.

02.4

O processamento da estrutura do discourse em contexto multimodal

Project associated with a CNPq Research Productivity Fellowship, level 1D, for 2024–2028. It investigates the role of acoustic and visual prosody in discourse-structure processing through behavioral tests and online methods. The research cycles on prosodic-boundary processing in discourse are experimental developments within this agenda.
