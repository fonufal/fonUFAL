<p>Maceió will host two international events dedicated to speech synthesis in September 2027: the <strong>14th ISCA Speech Synthesis Workshop (SSW14)</strong>, from <strong>6 to 8 September</strong>, and the <strong>Blizzard Challenge 2027</strong>, on <strong>9 September</strong>. SSW14 will be held as a satellite event of INTERSPEECH 2027, which will take place in São Paulo.</p>

<p>SSW is one of the main international forums for research and innovation in speech synthesis. Its programme brings together work on text-to-speech (TTS) technologies, prosody modelling, quality assessment, voice conversion, multilingual and cross-lingual aspects, speech resources, and related applications. The 2027 edition will feature the special theme <em>“Speaking in sub-tongues: embracing culture, heritage and diversity in Brazilian accents”</em>, with particular attention to linguistic diversity, Brazilian accents, spontaneous speech, and prosody.</p>

<p>The <strong>Blizzard Challenge 2027</strong>, held on the day following SSW14, will focus on the evaluation of speech synthesis systems. This edition will address accented zero-shot TTS for spontaneous Portuguese speech, involving varieties from São Paulo and Recife as well as European Portuguese.</p>

<p>Hosting SSW14 and the Blizzard Challenge in Maceió is part of <strong>fonUFAL’s</strong> initiatives in speech technologies and in strengthening research on Phonetics, Prosody, and speech synthesis. Bringing the events to Alagoas creates a direct forum for interaction among researchers, students, laboratories, and institutions working at the intersection of Linguistics, Artificial Intelligence, and speech processing.</p>

<p><strong>SSW14:</strong> 6–8 September 2027<br />
<strong>Blizzard Challenge 2027:</strong> 9 September 2027<br />
<strong>Location:</strong> Maceió, Alagoas, Brazil</p>
